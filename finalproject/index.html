About Hummi

I believe that children learn best when they are encouraged to explore the world at their own pace. My approach to childcare is centered on learning values like independence, fine and gross motor development exploration, cognitive socialization skills through play and interactions with other children. Here, we don't just 'babysit'—we spark curiosity through daily activities that build social skills, emotional intelligence, and a lifelong love for learning.

As a provider, I know that choosing the right care is one of the biggest decisions a parent can make. That’s why I view my role as a partnership with your family. At Hummi, you aren't just dropping off your child; you’re joining a community dedicated to your child’s safety, happiness, and growth. Education is about more than just numbers and letters; it’s about the values that shape a child’s character. We recognize that you are your child’s first and most important teacher. Our curriculum is designed to complement your home values, focusing on universal pillars like kindness, honesty, and curiosity. We maintain an ongoing dialogue with our families to ensure that the 'lessons' we teach through play and interaction align with the foundation you are building at home.

About Me

Hello! My name is Lizzeth Alia. I am an Early Childhood Educator with 17 years of experience in quality child care program. I am a mother of 4 wonderful kids. I am now providing services in my own home and I am looking for families who would like to partner with me in the education and care of their young children. My mission has always been to provide a second home for the children in my care. At Hummi, I combine my professional background with a nurturing environment where every child feels seen, heard, and loved.
