Discover Casa Grande

Casa Grande Neon Sign Park

Address: 408 N Sacaton St, Casa Grande, AZ 85122

The Neon Sign Park is a historic preservation of the lively town. It is operated and maintained by a non-profit organization. The park is part of the downtown area.

The Museum of Casa Grande

Address: 110 W Florence Blvd #4033, Casa Grande, AZ 85122

Founded in 1964, the Museum holds historic collections of artifacts, archives, photos, and properties from Casa Grande.

Casa Grande Ruins National Monument

Address: 1100 W. Ruins Drive Coolidge, AZ 85128

These ruins were said to be built around 1350 C.E. and is one of the largest prehistoric structures that were built by the ancestral Sonoran Desert people. In 1450, the Casa Grande was no longer in use.

Dave White Regional Park

Address: 2121 N Thornton Rd, Casa Grande, AZ 85122

The outdoor recreation park hold various amenities for families in the area. The park contains a Dave White Golf Course, Dave White Pond, playgrounds, and courts. The courts are available for pickleball, basketball, raquetball, and volleyball.

Casa Grance Community Recreation Center

Address: 1905 N Peart Rd, Casa Grande, AZ 85122

The recreational center contains a gym, basketball court, indoor track, gaming section, and various classes for families in the community. A small fee helps keep this place running and clean. Family packs available.

EOS Fitness

Address: 1214 E Florence Blvd #1, Casa Grande, AZ 85122

Newly opened fitness center that provides sauna, outdoor workout area, cinema machine area, and much more.

Promanade at Casa Grande

Address: 1005 N Promenade Pkwy, Casa Grande, AZ 85194

Promenade is an outdoor mall with many restaurants to enjoy, shopping stores, and events. Every Saturday there is a market for vendors.

Archery Range City of Casa Grande

Address: 400 E Rodeo Rd, Casa Grande, AZ 85122

Hidden gem for archers of many ages. Free range and spacious.
